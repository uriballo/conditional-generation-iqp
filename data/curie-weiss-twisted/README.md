Final results for C-IQP (TP) (`shared_twisted_parity`, trial 55) and
per-temperature IQP (TP) (`independent_twisted_parity`, trial 61), under the
parity protocol with the schedule constant a searched on [0.3, 0.7] and
κ = 0.4. Fit streams pair with the parity fits.

- `figure_data.json`: final curves and campaign receipts, written by `cw_twisted_bundle`.
- `kappa0_control.json`: the selected shared configuration refit with κ = 0 at a = selected, 0.3 and 0.7, ten fits each, written by `cw_kappa0_control`.

Protocols: `cw_twisted_protocol.json` and `cw_twisted_independent_protocol.json`.
