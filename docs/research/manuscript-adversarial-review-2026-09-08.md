## Findings corrected

1. **High — ancilla-only parameters were counted as added visible modeling capacity.** The general ancilla section and scalar configuration table called the full bank trainable, while the new follow-up correctly removed ancilla-only phases. Any ancilla-only diagonal unitary commutes to a local ancillary output unitary and cancels under the partial trace, regardless of input entanglement. For a complete two-local bank, the nominal stored count is N(N+1)/2; removing r(r+1)/2 inactive directions leaves at most n(n+1)/2+nr visible-affecting directions, before further encoder symmetries. Corrected the general section, table and CW resource comparison: the 18-qubit circuit stores 171 phases but 55 are ancilla-only, leaving at most 116; the 8-qubit comparator stores 36. State-preparation resources remain unmatched. No historical fit was altered or relabeled as trained with the reduced bank.
2. **Medium — classical appendix hard-coded a denominator of 1,000 despite the new data-size arms.** Replaced it by s+Kλ, explicitly distinguished original s=1,000 studies from s=100/300/1,000, and documented fresh follow-up seeds and counts. The runner already used the correct arm-specific denominator. Scoped every-condition improvement claims to the original zero-ancilla studies, so they cannot be read as applying to the new poor fits.
3. **Medium — numerical sampling qualification was incomplete.** The proposition previously allowed controlled numerical errors without specifying what an approximate sampler must control. It now explicitly assumes exact reference sampling, permits the stated RMS evaluation error, and notes that approximate sampling needs a separate weighted-observable bias bound. Small sampling TVD alone does not bound expectations of arbitrarily large ratio samples.
4. **Medium — different sources of uncertainty were easy to conflate.** Added that Gaussian/CW seed intervals condition on fixed data archives and measure run variability; Ashkin–Teller instead uses fresh data per paired seed. This agrees with exact_tvd_runner.prepare_data_archive (one archive per dataset/width). The three-seed CW ablation now suggests encoder–map dependence rather than implying a confirmatory paired interaction test based on differences of medians.
5. **Low — stale scope and a potentially overbroad MMD reading.** Updated the abstract and setup for the completed correlated-input ancilla study. Stated that characteristicness at fixed dimension does not imply polynomial-precision MMD controls TVD uniformly as dimension grows. The abstract includes the unresolved data–ancilla interaction without replacing it by a null-effect claim.

## Residual referee objections, not silently resolved

- **Novelty is the main scientific judgment.** The sampling-and-ratio interface is a specialization of existing tractable-state estimation and IQP MMD machinery. The paper explicitly attributes those ingredients. Its contribution should be judged as the conditional formulation, tractable encoder constructions and empirical limits; more benchmark seeds cannot by themselves establish conceptual novelty.
- **Benchmarks remain tractable and supplied encoders informative.** Gaussian/CW are exchangeable; Ashkin–Teller has a well-specified affine classical log-odds representation. The bond input includes the uncoupled law and coupling dependence. This is a methods demonstration, not a general-purpose modeling or advantage result. The classical wins are properly retained.
- **The data–ancilla interaction is unresolved.** Ten seeds and two isolated low-data bond failures give wide paired intervals. Neither no interaction nor equivalence is established, and parameter count, fixed update budget and substantial validation access prevent stronger causal/sample-complexity claims. No seed exclusion, retuning or replacement fit is justified by this review.
- **End-to-end scaling is not demonstrated.** Gaussian/CW checkpoint selection computes exact model distributions; the newer Ashkin–Teller selection uses sampled MMD. Exact evaluation and physical input preparation remain separate costs. The completed estimator precision/cost figures are still standalone; integrating a compact existing accuracy/cost figure would strengthen a submission, but does not require another experiment and is not necessary before supervisor review.
- **Public reproduction is not yet ready.** Raw frozen artifacts remain in local/remote ignored stores and archival deposition is incomplete. This is stated in Data Availability. A release package and authorship/funding confirmation remain author tasks before submission.
