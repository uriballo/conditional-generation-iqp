# Make the manuscript theory-led

The manuscript will be a mechanism-and-boundary paper: its primary claims are
that product-state conditioning preserves classically estimable IQP observable
training and that the resulting conditional family has characterizable
expressivity limits. The alternative empirical-first framing was rejected
because the present positive evidence is concentrated on one shell-structured
Target family, while the structured Bitstring, multidimensional, and strict
scaling studies expose important failures rather than broad competitiveness.
