# Cap the Ising study at 400 training runs

The complete non-shell campaign is capped at 400 full model-training runs and
released through Target-only calibration, a feasibility gate, a bounded pilot,
the 320-run frozen primary matrix, and—only after the primary passes—the
24-run Bounded Ising ancilla extension. The cap includes required baselines,
Separate-model banks, ancilla models, and Matched-capacity controls; failed
gates terminate or redirect the study, and no additional architecture or
hyperparameter ladder may consume runs without explicitly reopening the
publication plan.
