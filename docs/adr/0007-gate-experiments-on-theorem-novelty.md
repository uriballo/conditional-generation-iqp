# Gate new experiments on theorem novelty

No new major experimental campaign will begin until an internal adversarial
proof and prior-art audit classifies the proposed estimator and
conditional-expressivity results. This gate provisionally continues the
Quantum plan only if at least one correct central theorem plausibly clears the
journal's significance threshold; otherwise the project will redirect its
contribution or choose a less demanding venue before spending additional
compute. External significance is assessed separately after a full draft.
