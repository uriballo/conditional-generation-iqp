# Characterize both conditional possibility and obstruction

The theory-led manuscript will pair its observable-estimation result with a
conditional expressivity characterization: resource-scaled selector and
ancilla constructions provide upper results, while a fixed-width
broadcast-angle obstruction limits the practical 2-local family tested in the
experiments. The universality constructions will be subjected to a novelty and
proof audit and presented only at the strength not already implied by prior
work; they will not be attributed to the tested model.
