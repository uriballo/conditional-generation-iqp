# Freeze the ancilla-extension success rule

An improvement from ancilla marginalization may be claimed only if at least
one preregistered rung, \(m=3\) or \(m=5\), lowers paired median Held-out
Bitstring TVD relative to both the corresponding primary \(m=0\) model and its
Matched-capacity control, beats each comparator in at least 75% of
instance-seed-Held-out-Condition cells, and has no worse mean Held-out TVD than
\(m=0\) on either selected coupling instance. Both rungs must be reported
regardless of outcome. With only two coupling instances, this extension is
descriptive and cannot support a population-level performance claim.
