# Keep the Ising study a conditioning-mechanism benchmark

The PennyLane-Ising experiment compares fixed-circuit input conditioning,
Condition-modulated IQP phases, No-condition IQP, and Separate-model IQP
references against exact Targets. Classical generators are excluded from the
mandatory comparison because the finite Ising Targets and their Hamiltonians
are deliberately classically tractable; the study tests quantum-model design
constraints and makes no classical-performance or quantum-advantage claim.
