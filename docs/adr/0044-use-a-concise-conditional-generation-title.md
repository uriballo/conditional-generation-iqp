# Use a concise conditional-generation title

The working title is *Conditional Generation with Classically Trainable IQP
Circuits*. The title deliberately stays concise rather than enumerating the
finite-resource theorem and fixed-circuit deployment constraint. Those two
distinguishing features must be stated prominently in the Abstract and opening
contribution statement so that the shorter title does not blur the paper's
actual novelty. Retitling remains possible after external review.
