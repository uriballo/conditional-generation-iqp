# Require robust Ising interpolation beyond lookup

Genuine Ising interpolation requires three frozen outcomes against Oracle
adjacent-beta lookup: the aggregate paired bootstrap interval for the TVD
improvement is strictly positive, fixed-circuit evaluation wins at least 75%
of Held-out-Condition by coupling-instance cells, and no coupling instance has
worse mean Held-out Bitstring TVD than its Oracle. A favorable pooled mean or
selected midpoint cannot substitute for this rule.
