# Retain classically trainable terminology

The manuscript retains "classically trainable" in its headline terminology,
using the project's explicit operational definition: the specified Conditional
MMD loss and its gradients admit efficient unbiased classical estimation and
the model is optimized without quantum evaluations. The term does not claim
general convergence, immunity to barren plateaus, efficient classical sampling,
or quantum advantage; the estimator and gradient scaling benchmark must expose
this boundary prominently. The operational definition and exclusions must
appear on the first page, and Related Work must distinguish estimator
tractability from optimization-landscape trainability. Observed gradient
magnitudes do not veto this terminology because they concern a separate
landscape property rather than whether gradients can be estimated classically.
