# Gate Ising sharing on single-Condition fidelity

Before shared models are interpreted, sampled-MMD Separate models must fit the
canonical and one secondary coupling instance at the low, anchor, and high
Training inverse temperatures with median exact Bitstring TVD no greater than
0.25 and no cell above 0.35. Failure permits one exact-objective diagnostic to
classify estimator or family limitations, but no topology, Target-range, or
objective rescue; the shared Ising campaign then stops.
