# Freeze the manuscript claim hierarchy

The manuscript leads with the finite, characterizable multivariate response
spectrum induced when product-state Conditions drive a fixed IQP circuit. It
then establishes efficient classical estimation of the Pauli observables,
sampled Conditional MMD loss, and gradients used for training, which is the
paper's precise meaning of Classically trainable. Its primary empirical claim
is conditional on the frozen gates: on the Ising temperature family, the fixed
circuit retains a substantial fraction of the performance of reprogrammed
phase conditioning. Ancilla marginalization is presented only as a secondary
capacity extension. The manuscript makes no universality, quantum-advantage,
or general Landscape-trainability claim.
