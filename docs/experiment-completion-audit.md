## Active protocol boundaries

- Do not rerun the Gaussian or Curie--Weiss v3 core campaigns.
- Use only the v3 spin-flip input and calibrated Curie--Weiss knot map for the
  active result. The encoding family and knots were selected in development
  screens at training temperatures and frozen before the 80 production fits.
- Treat the v2 91-versus-92-phase comparison as a historical diagnostic. It
  compares two v2 product-input models and cannot explain or control the v3
  spin-flip result.
- Treat the two symmetry screens as post-core exploratory work. In particular,
  do not describe the radial-shell result as isolating the benefit of symmetry
  matching.
- Average conditions within each seed and use the deterministic 10,000-sample
  paired bootstrap for aggregate uncertainty.
- The final artifact is a local author-review package. No submission or upload
  is authorized.
