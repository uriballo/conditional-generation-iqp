# Claim changes for coauthor review

- Curie--Weiss v3, not v2, is now the active 80-fit primary result. It uses the
  spin-flip two-branch input and the frozen calibrated condition map.
- The abstract and main text no longer use the v2 matched-capacity comparison
  as evidence for v3. That comparison is retained in the supplement as a
  historical v2 product-input diagnostic.
- The two-block and radial-shell screens moved from the main text to the
  supplement and are labeled post-core exploratory work. The radial comparison
  is explicitly unequal because only its radial arm receives both condition
  coordinates.
- Curie--Weiss v3 is described as having no resolved fidelity loss relative to
  its condition-specific references in this finite campaign, rather than as
  erasing a general sharing cost.
- The contribution is input-state conditioning within the classically
  observable-trainable IQP construction, not the first conditional quantum
  generator.
- The general estimator statement applies to product inputs and bounds a
  second moment; it does not claim zero variance or guaranteed optimization.
- Ancillas are not claimed to cause the v3 improvement or to be necessary for
  multimodality or nonzero entropy.
- Gaussian evidence still shows a sharing--fidelity tradeoff and a condition
  coverage effect whose gain flattens after roughly five conditions.
- Universality, quantum advantage, thermal-state preparation, asymptotic
  estimator scaling, optimizer guarantees, and submission readiness are not
  claimed.
