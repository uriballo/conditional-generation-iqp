Without the learned circuit, the product-state encoder has full-distribution TVDs of $0.1180$, $0.4406$, and $0.8699$ at $\beta=0.4,1.0,1.6$, respectively; the spin-flip invariant encoder gives $0.2561$, $0.5536$, and $0.9002$. Input projection alone therefore does not improve the fit at these temperatures.
